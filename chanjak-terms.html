1. Who can use the App

Chanjak Studio Publisher is an internal tool used only by Chanjak Studio to manage its own Instagram and Threads account (chanjak_studio). It is not available to the public. (본 앱은 찬작스튜디오가 자사 계정 운영에만 쓰는 내부용 앱이며 일반에 제공되지 않습니다.)

2. What the App does

The App publishes Chanjak Studio's own posts, reads their insights, and searches public Threads posts by keyword so the owner can find people looking for a custom video and reply to them manually. The App does not post, like, follow, or send messages on other users' content automatically.
